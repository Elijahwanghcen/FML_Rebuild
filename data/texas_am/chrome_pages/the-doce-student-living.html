    Grab Your Space at The Doce!

    Find Your Space at The Doce!

    Students at Texas A&M have many options when choosing an off-campus apartment community, and we invite you to explore all that The Doce has to offer. Our fully furnished floor plans     feature two to four bedrooms, each with a private bathroom, ensuring comfort and privacy. Enjoy our modern boutique-style amenities, unique features, and convenient location to campus. At The Doce, everything you need for a comfortable student lifestyle is right here!

    Exciting community renovations are underway, and you’ll want to be among the first to experience everything the new Doce has to offer. Enjoy a redesigned clubhouse with brand-new study spaces, a refreshed fitness center, enhanced outdoor lounges, and so much more. With something for everyone, the updated Doce is sure to impress. Discover your Texas A&M home away from home today!


    Get to Know Our Floor Plan Options:


    Here at The Doce, we know how important it is to find the perfect place to call home during your college years. Whether you want to choose your roommates or match with new friends, we've got something for you! Take a look at our spacious floor plans and choose a 2-, 3-, or 4-bedroom apartment.

    🌟 PRO-TIP 🌟 Use the tabbed section on your application called Questionnaire to note if you are applying with friends and want to live together. For floor plan-specific selections, all roomies will need to make the same selection. For room-specific selections, our office team will work with you before signing your lease contract to ensure that each person is placed in their desired room preference.

    Interested in Current Term Housing? Please contact our team directly for more information.

    Pet Policy

    View our Pet Policy

    No animals are permitted anywhere in the bedroom, dwelling unit, apartment or the apartment community, at any time, without the Landlord’s written permission. Resident will be allowed to keep 1 dog or 1 cat as pets within their bedroom, dwelling unit or apartment subject to the following restrictions: (1) No aggressive dog breeds are permitted, including but not limited to, Pit bulls, (this includes American Staffordshire Terriers and Staffordshire Bull Terriers), Rottweilers, German Shepherds, Huskies, Alaskan Malamutes, Doberman Pinschers, Chow Chows, Great Danes, St. Bernards, and Akitas. (2) All other animals (including mammals, reptiles, birds, fish, rodents, and insects) are prohibited in the apartment or the apartment community. (3) Resident must provide Landlord with documentation that verifies that all animals have the required licenses and that all tags and inoculations are current. Landlord will authorize support and/or service animals for the Resident and Residents' guests and occupants in accordance with federal, state and local fair housing laws and regulations.
